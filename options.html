Settings

Where documents are saved and what they are called. Changes apply to documents saved from now on.

Inside Chrome's Downloads folder. To save somewhere else entirely, change the download location in Chrome's settings.

Use / for folders inside folders, such as {provider}/{year}.

Example

What you can use

{date}
2026-10-28, the document's date
{date:mmm yyyy}
Oct 2026. Build your own from yyyy, yy, mmmm, mmm, mm and dd
{year} {month} {day}
2026, 10 and 28
{provider}
The site's name, such as Chase Business
{title}
What the site calls it, which can include the account
[ - {title}]
Square brackets: left out when a field inside has no value
