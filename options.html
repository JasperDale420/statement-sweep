Sites

Include in Sweeps

A site switched off here is left out of every sweep and the toolbar count. Each time you open Statement Sweep, the sites that need updating are ticked for you; untick any you want to skip that time.

Dismissed Gaps

A possible gap you dismissed in the popup is not flagged again. A gap somewhere new still is.

Card Offers

Add When Syncing

Off unless you turn it on. After a site's statements are saved, Statement Sweep opens its card offers in the same tab and presses each offer's own add button, nothing else. It never buys, applies, enrolls or accepts terms.

Offers Seen
Open Card Offers

Forgetting clears this list. Offers already on your cards stay on them, and none is pressed twice: each site shows them as added.

History

Already Saved

Statement Sweep remembers which documents it has saved, so it never saves one twice. Move that record to another copy of Statement Sweep, such as the one from the Chrome Web Store. Your chosen folder does not come along; choose it again there.

Run Logs

What each site did in a sweep, and why it stopped, for fixing a site that does not save. With a folder chosen, each run's log is in a Logs folder beside each site's documents. Without one, the latest lines are kept in Chrome, and Export Logs saves them as one text file. Logs never leave your computer, and long numbers in them show only their last 4 digits.
